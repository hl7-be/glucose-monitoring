BeModelDiagnosticReportDiabetes
BeModelDiagnosticReportDiabetes / businessIdentifier
Equivalent To DiagnosticReport.identifier (At least one identifier is required, including the UUID slice with system fixed to be-ns-diagnostic-report-diabetes. Slicing is open, so other business identifiers can be added.)
BeModelDiagnosticReportDiabetes / recordedDate
Equivalent To DiagnosticReport.extension:recorded-date
BeModelDiagnosticReportDiabetes / observationPeriod
​Equivalent To DiagnosticReport.effective[x] (effective[x] is restricted to Period; start and end are both mandatory.)
BeModelDiagnosticReportDiabetes / patient
Equivalent To DiagnosticReport.subject (Reference(BePatient). For GDPR reasons, sensor suppliers may not use the NISS of their users in external communication.)
BeModelDiagnosticReportDiabetes / status
Equivalent To DiagnosticReport.status (Bound to BeVSDiabetesReportStatus. The value is assigned according to the business rule on % Data Captured.)
BeModelDiagnosticReportDiabetes / recorder
Equivalent To DiagnosticReport.extension:recorder (Reference(BePractitioner | BeOrganization). The professional is identified by INAMI or national register number, the organization by INAMI approval or company number.)
BeModelDiagnosticReportDiabetes / interpreter
Equivalent To DiagnosticReport.resultsInterpreter (Reference(BePractitionerRole | BePractitioner). Not provided by the report producer; added by the interpreting care provider.)
BeModelDiagnosticReportDiabetes / category
Equivalent To DiagnosticReport.category (Bound (preferred) to BeVSDiabetesReportCategory.)
BeModelDiagnosticReportDiabetes / deviceType
Equivalent To DiagnosticReport.extension:device (valueCodeableConcept bound (extensible) to BeVSDiabetesDeviceType. The individual sensor device is mentioned in the measurement observation.)
BeModelDiagnosticReportDiabetes / code
Equivalent To DiagnosticReport.code (Bound to BeVSDiabetesReportCode (e.g. 439926003 Ambulatory continuous glucose monitoring of interstitial tissue fluid).)
BeModelDiagnosticReportDiabetes / derivedObservations
Equivalent To DiagnosticReport.result (Reference(BeObservationDiabetes).)
BeModelDiagnosticReportDiabetes / measuredDiabetesObservations
Equivalent To DiagnosticReport.result (Reference(BeObservationDiabetes). Shares DiagnosticReport.result with the derived observations; there is no separate slice yet.)
BeModelDiagnosticReportDiabetes / note
Equivalent To DiagnosticReport.extension:note (Not provided by the report producer; added by the interpreting care provider.)
BeModelDiagnosticReportDiabetes / document
Equivalent To DiagnosticReport.presentedForm (contentType fixed to application/pdf.)
