Logical Model: BeModelDiagnosticReportDiabetes

Official URL: https://www.ehealth.fgov.be/standards/fhir/glucose-monitoring/StructureDefinition/BeModelDiagnosticReportDiabetes Version: 1.0.0

NameFlagsCard.TypeDescription & Constraints    Filter:



BeModelDiagnosticReportDiabetes 0..* Base BeModelDiagnosticReportDiabetes
BeModelDiagnosticReportDiabetes.businessIdentifier 1..* Identifier Business identifier of the report
BeModelDiagnosticReportDiabetes.recordedDate 1..1 dateTime Date of the last update of the report
BeModelDiagnosticReportDiabetes.observationPeriod 1..1 Period From date to date corresponding to the period covered by the report. Typically 14 to 30 days. See business rules
BeModelDiagnosticReportDiabetes.patient 1..1 Reference(BePatient) The patient that this report is about
BeModelDiagnosticReportDiabetes.status 1..1 code Report status, by default “Final”. The value is assigned according to the business rule on “% Data Captured”
Binding: BeVSDiabetesReportStatus (required)
BeModelDiagnosticReportDiabetes.recorder 1..1 Reference(BePractitioner | BeOrganization) The healthcare professional (INAMI number / national register number) or organization (INAMI approval number / company number) that encodes the information and takes responsibility for its content
BeModelDiagnosticReportDiabetes.interpreter 0..1 Reference(BePractitionerRole | BePractitioner) Healthcare provider (INAMI number / national register number) who added an interpretation to the report (e.g. endocrinologist, general practitioner). Not provided by the producer of the report
BeModelDiagnosticReportDiabetes.category 1..* CodeableConcept Category of the report. At least one category must be assigned
Binding: BeVSDiabetesReportCategory (preferred)
BeModelDiagnosticReportDiabetes.deviceType 0..1 CodeableConcept Identification number assigned by INAMI to the type of sensor model used to create the observations on which the report is based. The individual sensor device is mentioned in the measurement observation
Binding: BeVSDiabetesDeviceType (extensible)
BeModelDiagnosticReportDiabetes.code 1..1 CodeableConcept Report code - indicating the type of procedure that leads to the production of the primary values of this report, i.e. 439926003 | Ambulatory continuous glucose monitoring of interstitial tissue fluid
Binding: BeVSDiabetesReportCode (required)
BeModelDiagnosticReportDiabetes.derivedObservations 0..* Observation List of references to “derived values” type observations (a dozen derived observations) mentioned in the report. These observations are part of the diagnostic report
BeModelDiagnosticReportDiabetes.measuredDiabetesObservations 0..* Observation References to the measured diabetes observations (a few thousand measurements) used to produce the analysis
BeModelDiagnosticReportDiabetes.note 0..* Annotation Report comments in free text format. Not provided by the producer of the report, but by the healthcare provider doing the interpretation
BeModelDiagnosticReportDiabetes.document 0..* Binary PDF document containing the complete report (text, graphics, deduced observations, conclusions, etc.)
